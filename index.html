About

Acid Fairies is an independent brand born in Poznan, Poland in 2026. We create in hope to make you feel as amazing and beautiful as you are!!

We believe that fashion is a way to express your individuality, your personality and everything that makes you, you. We want to create a space where everyone is comfortable and feels beautiful and comfortable in what they choose to wear. We hope to create products that last and will bring you joy for many years.

Q&A

Every order usually takes up to a week to finish, however if you order more pieces it can take a little longer, up to 3 weeks. We'll notify you when your order is ready to be shipped.

Standard shipping is $25. For all orders over $250 the shipping is free.

Delivery to the EU and UK shouldn't take longer than 3 days to arrive. To the US standard delivery can take up to a week.

Since we make all our items on commission, you can't cancel or modify your order after you've received an email that we've began making your items. You can send us an email or DM us on Instagram before that and then you'll be able to modify your order or receive a refund.

If you ordered a piece with customisations you can't return it. We value the satisfaction of our clients a lot, so please contact us on Instagram or via email and let us know about problems you had and we'll do our best to resolve the issue so you can be happy with your piece.

To return a piece without any customisations, DM us on Instagram or email us and we'll get back to you and you'll be able to return the piece.

Due to hygiene reasons, underwear, such as bralettes, thongs and panties are non-returnable.

We accept PayPal, Apple Pay and credit card payments. After you add products to the cart and want to complete your order you'll be directed to PayPal.

All the specific information about taking care of your pieces will be delivered with each one.

Here's a little reminder:

For sweatshirts, hoodies and sweatpants:

1. Machine wash in cold temperatures.

2. Use a gentle program.

3. Dry flat.

4. Do not bleach.

5. Iron if necessary.

For underwear, tops and skirts:

1. Machine wash in cold temperatures.

2. Use a gentle program.

3. Dry flat.

4. Do not bleach.

5. Do not iron.
